# Selling on TestMandi — A Seller's Guide

This guide walks you through everything from creating your seller account to publishing your first MCQ test for sale on testmandi.in.

---

## 1. Register as a Seller

1. Go to **testmandi.in**.
2. Click **"Log in / Register"** in the top-right corner.
3. On the sign-up screen, select **"Teacher / Institute (Seller)"** (not Buyer or Advertiser).
4. Fill in:
   - **Contact person name** — your own name
   - **Institute / brand name** — the name shown to buyers as the seller (e.g. "Apex Physics Institute")
   - **Email** — use a real address; you'll need to verify it
   - **Phone number**
   - **Password** — at least 8 characters
5. Click **Register**.

You're logged in immediately after registering.

## 2. Verify Your Email

- After registering, you'll see an amber banner at the top of the site: *"Please verify your email address."*
- Check your inbox for an email from TestMandi with a **Verify email** link.
- Click it — you'll see a confirmation screen, and the banner will disappear.
- Didn't get it? Click **"Resend email"** in the banner.

*(Verifying isn't required to use the site, but it's good practice — do it early.)*

## 3. Set Up How You'll Get Paid

Before you can withdraw any earnings, add your payout details:

1. Click **"Seller Studio"** in the top navigation.
2. Find the **"Bank & payouts"** section.
3. Add at least one of the following:
   - **UPI ID** (e.g. `yourname@upi`) — fastest to set up
   - **Upload your UPI QR code image** — a photo/screenshot of your existing GPay, PhonePe, or bank app QR code
   - **Full bank details** — account holder name, bank name, IFSC code, and account number (entered twice to confirm)
4. Click **Save payout details**.

You can add more than one of these — having a UPI ID or QR code makes it much easier for the admin team to pay you quickly.

## 4. Create Your First Test

1. In **Seller Studio**, click **"+ Create test"**.
2. A 3-step wizard opens:

### Step 1 — Basics
- **Test title** — be specific, e.g. "NEET Biology — Genetics & Evolution Sprint"
- **Category** — choose from the list (NEET, JEE Main, UPSC CSE, etc.)
- **Duration** — how many minutes candidates get to complete it. Once a candidate starts, the test **automatically submits** when this time runs out.
- **Price** — what buyers pay, in ₹
- **Description** — a short summary of what the test covers and who it's for

### Step 2 — Questions
For each question, fill in:
- The question text
- **4 answer options**
- Which option is correct (select the radio button next to it)
- A **topic tag** (e.g. "Genetics," "Mechanics") — this powers the topic-wise breakdown buyers see in their score report
- An optional **explanation** shown to buyers after they answer

Click **"+ Add question"** to add more. You need at least one fully-filled-in question to publish.

**Adding questions faster — three shortcuts:**
- **Upload CSV** — if you already have questions in a spreadsheet, export it as a CSV with columns: `question, option1, option2, option3, option4, correct (A/B/C/D or 1-4), topic, explanation`, and upload the whole file at once.
- **Generate with AI** — type a topic (e.g. "Photosynthesis"), pick how many questions you want and an optional difficulty, and the platform's AI writes a full set of questions for you in seconds.
- **Scan question paper** — take a photo or scan of a printed question paper you already have, and the AI reads it directly and extracts the multiple-choice questions automatically — no manual retyping.

However you add them, **review every question carefully before publishing** — you're responsible for their accuracy, especially anything generated or scanned.

### Step 3 — Review & Publish
- Check the summary of your test.
- Click **Publish test**.

Your test is now live in the Marketplace immediately.

## 5. (Optional) Bundle Tests Together

If you have 2 or more published tests, you can package them at a discount:

1. In Seller Studio, click **"Bundle tests"**.
2. Give the bundle a title and description.
3. Select which of your tests to include.
4. Set a bundle price (lower than the sum of the individual prices, to show buyers real savings).
5. Publish.

## 6. Promote Your Test

- Every test and bundle has a **share icon** on its card.
- Click it to **copy a direct link** to that specific test, or **share it on WhatsApp** — the link opens straight to that test with a "Shared with you" spotlight, not just the general homepage.

## 7. Track Sales & Earnings

Back in Seller Studio, you'll see:
- **Gross sales**, **your earnings** (after the platform's share), **platform fee**, and **units sold**
- A row for every test/bundle showing how many times it's sold and what you've earned from it

## 8. Withdraw Your Earnings

1. In the **Bank & payouts** panel, your **available balance** is shown.
2. Click **Withdraw**.
3. If your account has real bank-transfer payouts enabled, this happens automatically.
4. Otherwise, your request goes to the TestMandi admin team for manual processing (via your UPI/QR/bank details) — you'll see the status update from *Pending* to *Completed* once paid.

## 9. Refer & Earn — a Second Income Stream

Every seller gets a personal referral code, shown in the **"Refer & earn"** panel in Seller Studio (just below Bank & Payouts). You earn a flat **₹200 cash bonus**, credited straight to your payout balance, in two ways:

- **Refer another seller** — you earn ₹200 the moment they publish their **first** test.
- **Refer a student or buyer** — you earn ₹200 the moment they complete their **first** purchase.

To share your code, use the **Copy invite link** or **Share on WhatsApp** buttons in the panel. Every account that registers using your code counts toward your referral total, and each bonus you earn shows up in a running history with who triggered it.

Referral bonuses are **on top of your normal sales earnings** — they're not reduced by the platform's profit-split percentage, since they're funded entirely by TestMandi as a thank-you for helping the marketplace grow.

---

### Tips for a Strong Listing

- Use a clear, specific title — buyers search by keyword.
- Tag each question's topic accurately — it's what makes your score reports genuinely useful to buyers, and useful reports lead to better ratings.
- Keep an eye on your rating — tests with **more than 10 ratings averaging below 3 stars are automatically hidden** from the marketplace, so quality matters.
- A well-priced bundle is often an easy way to increase what a single buyer spends with you.
